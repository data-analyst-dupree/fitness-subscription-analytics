# Fitness-Subscription-Analytics

## Customer Cohort Analysis:
New customer count is steadily rising from month to month, showing a strong marketing campaign. However, there is a trend around the 3rd month since purchase marker. Its seems that almost 50% of customers cancel their subscriptions after only 3 months. 

## Revenue Forecast:
Revenue shows signs of seasonality peaking in the November/December months, and declining in January. Revenue forecast estimates around $127,002 in revenue over the next 12 months.
